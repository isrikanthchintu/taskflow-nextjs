# Stakeholder Map — Trello-Style Task Board

## Key Stakeholders and Interests

| Stakeholder | Interest | Decision-maker / Influencer | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| You (sole user and developer) | A Trello-style board that is more visual, more usable, and more portfolio-worthy than the current flat task list | Decision-maker | [Q2], [Q4] |
| Anyone you show the board to (portfolio/demo audience) | Sees a polished, realistic-looking Kanban UI; does not manage tasks on it | Influencer (informal, no active editing role) | [Q2] |

## Decision-Makers vs. Influencers

- **Decision-maker**: you — the sole person who manages tasks and decides
  what the board should do [Q2].
- **Influencer**: an outside viewing audience, whose reaction to the UI may
  shape priorities (for example, favoring a "portfolio-worthy" look), but
  who does not use or edit the board themselves [Q2], [Q4].

## Communication Requirements

None — this is a single-person effort with no other active stakeholders to
report to or coordinate with, so no reporting cadence or communication
requirement applies [Q2].

## Assumptions & Open Questions

None.
