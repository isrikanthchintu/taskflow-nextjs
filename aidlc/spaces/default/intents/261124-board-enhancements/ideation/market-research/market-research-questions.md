# Market Research Questions

Context: Intent Capture settled that the board becomes a daily-use task manager, stays browser-only with export/import, and that I study Trello, Notion, Linear, Asana, Jira and GitHub Projects, then propose a ranked shortlist for you to choose from (intent-statement.md, Q1, Q4, Q8).

## Q1. [Product owner] Which core board capabilities matter most to you? (select all that apply)

Context: all six products let you shape the board itself and the cards on it. These are the core groups I found; your picks decide what ranks highest in the shortlist.

A. Custom lists: add, rename, reorder, archive and delete lists (Trello, Asana sections, Jira columns)
B. Richer cards: labels, checklists with progress, due-date states such as overdue and due soon, card detail view (Trello, Asana subtasks)
C. Finding things: filters, sort, and group-by on the board (Notion, GitHub Projects, Jira quick filters)
D. Several boards, each with its own lists and theme (Trello, Notion)
X. Other (please specify)

[Answer]: A, B, C, D (all four)

A. Keyboard shortcuts and a command menu such as Ctrl+K (Linear, GitHub Projects)
